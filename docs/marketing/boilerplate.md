## Long bio (~150 words)

> Zagros Dataware is a process and design consultancy registered in Yerevan,
> Armenia, built around a simple premise: the right discipline applied at the
> right speed beats a single playbook applied everywhere. For enterprises and
> public institutions, that means BPM and BPMS implementation, Agile-BPM
> iterative delivery, BABOK-aligned business analysis, and ERP consulting —
> structure where structure protects the organization. For founders and product
> teams, it means Lean Startup's Build-Measure-Learn loop, Google Design
> Sprints, Design Thinking, and Strategyzer's Value Proposition Design — speed
> where speed saves the runway. For local businesses, it means process-minded
> web design built around how customers actually discover, decide, and buy, not
> a generic template. Delivered work spans freight and logistics, industrial
> manufacturing, and independent music, among others. The throughline across all
> three: evidence over guesswork.
